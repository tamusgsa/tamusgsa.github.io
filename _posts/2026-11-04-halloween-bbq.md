The SGSA is holding its annual Halloween BBQ Party at  [Tiffany Park](<https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://www.bryantx.gov/parks-facilities/details/?facilityId=TNT-COB-00000266-53EE8B15-5074-47B7-9D1E-74C4762408D9__;!!KwNVnqRv!HaG8uyPF5WDz1g94baooFWiW1YaAt1lVY95NxfWP1Lzd1dhYCMuyEA8DejBylhqGqClg3wcFiGrZ5Fpdy6bJbSfl8at1_w$>) Pavilion A on Saturday, November 7th, from 4:00 to 7:00 PM.
 

Tiffany Park offers a range of amenities including volleyball and basketball courts, space for soccer, cricket, frisbees, and playgrounds for children, and is pet-friendly. We hope all of you will join us for an enjoyable evening of BBQ and outdoor games.  

 

Please RSVP using this [form](<https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://forms.gle/Hnw24ug2ZYbCrgtC7__;!!KwNVnqRv!HaG8uyPF5WDz1g94baooFWiW1YaAt1lVY95NxfWP1Lzd1dhYCMuyEA8DejBylhqGqClg3wcFiGrZ5Fpdy6bJbScZ2Gm8rw$>) by 5:00 PM on Friday, October 30th, to assist us in preparing for the event. 



If you're planning to attend the Halloween BBQ and you either need a ride or can offer a ride, please fill out the rideshare [form](<https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Nov4-EGllsOt3icKl1Nu8ocJLpNz49S7hxsme8wW49A/edit?usp=sharing__;!!KwNVnqRv!HaG8uyPF5WDz1g94baooFWiW1YaAt1lVY95NxfWP1Lzd1dhYCMuyEA8DejBylhqGqClg3wcFiGrZ5Fpdy6bJbScZUAl1Eg$>) (you should all have access to edit the form). This form is meant to help you connect with others in the department to coordinate transportation.
